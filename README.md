Meme & Creator Platform

Product Strategy | Business Analysis | Financial Modelling

Overview

This project explores the design and commercial viability of a Nigerian meme and short-video platform that combines content creation, social engagement, creator visibility, and user rewards.

The case study demonstrates how I transformed an initial business idea into a structured product concept by evaluating its monetisation model, reward system, financial sustainability, and growth strategy.

Key Features

- Meme and short-video creation.
- Paid creator verification and membership.
- Structured reward systems for creators and users.
- Multiple revenue streams, including subscriptions, content creation fees, and advertising.
- A proposed strategy for acquiring the first 100,000 users.

Analysis & Approach

- Evaluated the product concept and its business model.
- Analysed monetisation options and reward-related financial risks.
- Developed illustrative three-year financial scenarios.
- Refined the reward structure to improve cost predictability.
- Proposed a user acquisition and growth strategy.
- Identified key assumptions requiring validation before launch.

Skills Demonstrated

Product Strategy · Business Analysis · Financial Modelling · Quantitative Analysis · Market Research · Revenue Modelling · Growth Strategy · Critical Thinking

Project Status

Independent Product Concept & Business Case Study.
